The Retention Lever Most Founders Completely Ignore

Everyone talks about comp and equity. Point 2 is the cheap thing that actually moves the needle.

Compensation keeps people from leaving. It rarely makes them want to stay.

POINT 2:
Teams that notice and name small wins out loud build a kind of loyalty that a raise alone doesn't buy.

Founders spend a lot of energy on comp and equity to retain people, and not nearly enough on something that costs nothing: actually noticing the small wins out loud.

The teams with the strongest retention aren't the highest-paid ones. They're the ones where wins - even small ones - get named, not just logged.
