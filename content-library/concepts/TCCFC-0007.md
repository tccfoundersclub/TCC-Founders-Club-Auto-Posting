INTRO:
Big networking events feel productive. Point 2 is why they rarely are.

A room of 200 people gives you 200 shallow conversations, if you're fast.

POINT 2:
A table of 8 gives you one real one, and real conversations are what turn into relationships that matter a year later.

The founders who build genuinely useful networks stopped optimizing for room size a long time ago.

The founders with genuinely useful networks aren't the ones who go to the most events. They're the ones who've figured out that scale and depth trade off against each other, and picked depth.
