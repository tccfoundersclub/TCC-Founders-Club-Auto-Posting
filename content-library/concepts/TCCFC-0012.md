TOPIC:
Solo founders lose time to decisions that would take minutes with a thinking partner

HOOK:
The Hidden Tax of Deciding Everything Alone

INTRO:
Solo founders wear it as a badge. Point 2 is what it's quietly costing.

POINT 1:
A decision that takes ten minutes to talk through with someone else can take days to make alone, just from the looping.

POINT 2:
It's not that solo founders make worse decisions - it's that they make the same decisions slower, with more doubt along the way.

POINT 3:
The solo founders who move fastest have usually built an informal thinking-partner habit, even without a co-founder.

CAPTION:
Being a solo founder gets worn like a badge of independence. What it actually costs is speed - not because the decisions are worse, but because there's nobody to talk them through with in real time.

A choice that would take ten minutes with a thinking partner can take three days of internal looping alone. Not because it's a hard decision. Because there's no one to say "yeah, obviously, go with option two" and end the loop.

The solo founders who move fastest aren't the ones who trust their gut more. They've built an informal version of a co-founder - a call, a group chat, someone who'll give them the outside read in minutes, not days.
