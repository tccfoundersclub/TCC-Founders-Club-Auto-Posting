CAPTION:
"The market won't support a higher price" is the reason founders give for not raising prices. Often, they haven't actually tested it.

Underneath the market explanation is usually a more personal one: raising the price and getting a no feels like personal rejection, not a business data point. So the price stays low, and the fear stays untested.

The founders who price confidently have done one specific thing - they've separated "what does the business need" from "what if someone doesn't like me for asking." Those are two different questions, and only one of them should set your prices.
