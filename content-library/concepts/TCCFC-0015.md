TOPIC:
Always-be-closing sales culture burns out founder-led sales

HOOK:
"Always Be Closing" Is Quietly Burning Out Founder-Led Sales

INTRO:
It sounds like discipline. Point 2 is what it actually does to a founder over time.

POINT 1:
Treating every conversation as a potential close turns founders into people prospects start to avoid.

POINT 2:
The best founder-led salespeople aren't always closing - they're mostly just genuinely curious, and closing follows naturally from that.

POINT 3:
Burnout in founder-led sales usually isn't about volume. It's about performing "always on" instead of actually being interested.

CAPTION:
"Always be closing" sounds like discipline. In practice, for a lot of founder-led sales, it's a slow-motion burnout machine.

Treating every conversation like a potential close changes how you show up - people can feel it, and it makes them a little more guarded, not less. The founders who sell well over years, not just quarters, aren't running that script. They're genuinely curious about the person in front of them, and the close follows from that, not from technique.

If sales calls are draining you, the fix might not be a better script. It might be dropping the performance underneath it.
