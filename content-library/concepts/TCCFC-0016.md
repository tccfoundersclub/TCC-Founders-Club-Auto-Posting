TOPIC:
A structured mastermind beats a casual founder friend group for real progress

HOOK:
A Founder Friend Group and a Founder Mastermind Are Not the Same Thing

INTRO:
Both feel like community. Point 2 is the difference that actually matters.

POINT 1:
A friend group gives you comfort. A mastermind gives you accountability. Most founders only have the first one.

POINT 2:
Comfort feels good in the moment and rarely changes your next decision. Structure does, even when it's uncomfortable.

POINT 3:
The founders making the fastest progress usually have both - but if they had to pick one, they'd pick the structured room.

CAPTION:
A founder friend group and a founder mastermind can look identical from the outside - same people, same group chat, same dinners. The difference is what happens inside the room.

A friend group gives you comfort: people who get it, who'll commiserate, who make the hard days feel less lonely. That matters. But comfort rarely changes what you actually do next.

A structured room - real accountability, real questions, real follow-up on what you said you'd do last month - changes decisions. It's less comfortable and more useful.

Most founders have plenty of the first. Almost none have enough of the second.
