HOOK:
"We'll Hire When We Scale" Is Usually Backwards

INTRO:
It sounds like discipline. Point 2 is why it often just delays the inevitable, at a worse time.

POINT 1:
Waiting for the "right time" to hire often means hiring in a panic, once the gap is already hurting.

POINT 2:
The right senior hire, made slightly early, creates the capacity that makes scaling possible - not the other way around.

POINT 3:
The founders who grow smoothly usually hired the key role a little before they felt fully ready for it.

CAPTION:
"We'll hire for that when we scale" sounds financially responsible. Often, it just means you'll hire the role in a panic, three months after the gap started hurting, instead of calmly, before it did.

The right hire made slightly early doesn't just fill a gap - it creates capacity you didn't have, which is often what actually unlocks the next stage of growth. Waiting for scale to justify the hire gets the sequence backwards.

The founders who grow smoothly aren't the ones who waited the longest to hire. They're the ones who hired a beat before they felt fully ready.
