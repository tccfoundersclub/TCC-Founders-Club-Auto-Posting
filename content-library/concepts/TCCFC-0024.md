TOPIC:
Founders who raise a second round successfully share a pattern

HOOK:
The Founders Who Raise a Clean Second Round Did One Thing Differently the First Time

INTRO:
Everyone focuses on the pitch for round two. Point 2 is what actually set it up.

POINT 1:
A clean second raise is mostly decided by how the first round's money was used and reported on, not by round two's deck.

POINT 2:
Investors talk to each other. A founder who over-communicated and under-promised in round one gets an easier round two.

POINT 3:
The founders who raise smoothly twice treated the first round's investors like a long relationship, not a closed transaction.

CAPTION:
Everyone obsesses over the pitch for a second raise. The pitch matters far less than what happened between round one and round two.

Investors talk. A founder who communicated proactively, hit what they said they'd hit (or explained clearly when they didn't), and treated the first round as the start of a relationship - not a closed transaction - walks into round two with a much easier conversation.

The second raise isn't won in the deck. It's won in the eighteen months before you even start building it.
