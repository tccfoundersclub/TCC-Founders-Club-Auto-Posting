Over-indexing on same-industry peers misses cross-industry insight

The Best Idea for Your Startup Might Be Sitting in a Completely Different Industry

INTRO:
Founders default to peers in their own space. Point 2 is what that habit misses.

Talking only to people in your industry means hearing the same handful of ideas everyone else already has too.

POINT 2:
Cross-industry founders often see your problem from an angle nobody inside your space has tried, because they're not boxed in by "how it's done."

POINT 3:
The founders with the sharpest ideas usually have at least a few relationships well outside their own category.

It's natural to build your closest founder relationships with people in your own industry - shared language, shared problems, instant rapport. It's also a trap.

The founders with the sharpest, most original thinking usually keep at least a few relationships well outside their own lane, on purpose.
