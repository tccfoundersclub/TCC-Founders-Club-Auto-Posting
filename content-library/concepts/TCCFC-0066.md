HOOK:
Your Company Is Growing. Are You?

INTRO:
Founders pour all growth energy into the business. Point 2 is what quietly gets left behind.

POINT 1:
It's easy to measure company growth in revenue and headcount. Personal growth has no dashboard, so it's easy to ignore.

POINT 2:
A founder who stops learning while the company scales eventually becomes the ceiling on how far it can go.

The founders who keep growing personally treat their own development as seriously as any other line item in the business.

CAPTION:
Company growth is easy to track - revenue charts, headcount, retention. Personal growth doesn't show up on a dashboard, so it's the thing founders most quietly let slide while everything else scales.

That's a problem, because a company can only grow as far as its founder does. A founder who stopped developing two years ago becomes, eventually, the actual ceiling on the business - not the market, not the product, but the person at the top who stopped stretching.

The founders who keep scaling well treat their own growth as a real priority, not an afterthought - reading, rooms, mentors, honest feedback - the same seriousness they'd give any other part of the business.
