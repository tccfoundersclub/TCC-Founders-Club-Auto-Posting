TOPIC:
Founders underestimate how much genuine human connection (not networking) sustains them

HOOK:
Networking Sustains Your Business. Connection Sustains You.

INTRO:
Founders treat every relationship as strategic. Point 2 is what that framing misses.

POINT 1:
A relationship evaluated purely for its usefulness rarely feels nourishing, even when it's genuinely valuable professionally.

POINT 2:
Founders who only build "strategic" relationships often end up surrounded by people and still feel alone.

POINT 3:
The founders who stay resilient long-term have some relationships with zero professional agenda at all - just genuine connection.

CAPTION:
A lot of founder relationships get built and maintained with one eye on usefulness - what can this person do for the business. Even when those relationships are genuinely valuable, they rarely feel nourishing in the way founders actually need.

You can be surrounded by useful contacts and still feel completely alone, because usefulness and connection aren't the same thing, and a founder's nervous system knows the difference even when their calendar doesn't.

The founders who stay resilient over the long run have kept at least some relationships with zero professional agenda - people they'd talk to even if neither of them ever built anything again.
