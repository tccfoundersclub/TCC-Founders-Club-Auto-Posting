TOPIC:
Founders wait for a 'good enough' moment to ask for help that never arrives

HOOK:
There's No Version of "Bad Enough to Finally Ask for Help" That Actually Feels Bad Enough

INTRO:
Founders set an internal bar for when asking is justified. Point 2 is why that bar keeps moving.

POINT 1:
Founders tell themselves they'll ask for help "if it gets really bad" - and when it does, it still doesn't feel bad enough to justify asking.

POINT 2:
The bar for "bad enough to ask" keeps quietly moving, because admitting you've hit it feels like admitting failure.

POINT 3:
The founders who get support in time are the ones who stopped waiting for a threshold and just asked earlier than felt comfortable.

CAPTION:
A lot of founders have an unspoken rule: I'll ask for help if it gets really bad. The problem is that the bar for "really bad" keeps quietly moving, because reaching it would mean admitting something uncomfortable, and that discomfort always finds a reason to push the line further out.

There's no moment that actually clears the bar cleanly. It always still feels like it's not quite bad enough, like other founders have it worse, like asking now would be premature.

The founders who get support in time are the ones who stopped waiting for a clean threshold and just asked earlier than felt fully justified. Earlier always turns out to have been the right time.
