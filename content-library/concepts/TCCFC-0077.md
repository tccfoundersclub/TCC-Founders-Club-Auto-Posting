Two founders having a rough patch usually assume it stays between them - handled in private, invisible to the team. It almost never actually is.

Unresolved tension leaks into every shared meeting, every joint decision, every moment the two of you are in a room together. Teams are unusually sensitive to instability at the top, and they read the strain even when nobody's explained what's going on.

The founder pairs who protect their team's stability deal with tension directly, between themselves, instead of letting it quietly bleed into the culture as unexplained unease.

If something's off between you and your co-founder, your team probably already knows. Address it before it becomes the atmosphere.
