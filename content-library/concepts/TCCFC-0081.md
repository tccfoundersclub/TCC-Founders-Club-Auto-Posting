CAPTION:
Founders often avoid asking their network for anything - a discount, an intro, a favor - out of fear it'll cost the relationship. In reality, most people are far more willing to help than that fear suggests. The fear of asking is usually bigger than the actual risk of it.

What actually damages a relationship isn't the ask itself. It's how it's framed - a vague, guilt-tinted request that makes saying no feel awkward puts real pressure on the other person, whether they help or not.

A specific, respectful ask with an easy, comfortable "no" built into it rarely costs anything, even when the answer is no. People remember being asked well more than they remember being asked at all.
