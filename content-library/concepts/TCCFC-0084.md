TOPIC:
"We need more leads" is often a symptom, not the real problem

HOOK:
"We Just Need More Leads" Is Rarely the Actual Problem

INTRO:
It's the easiest diagnosis to reach for. Point 2 is what it usually hides.

POINT 1:
More leads into a broken conversion process just produces more people falling through the same broken process, faster.

POINT 2:
Founders reach for "more leads" because it's an external fix - it doesn't require looking at anything uncomfortable internally.

POINT 3:
The founders who actually fix growth start by asking why the leads they already have aren't converting, before spending anything to get more.

CAPTION:
"We just need more leads" is the easiest diagnosis a founder can reach for, because it points outward - get more traffic, run more ads, do more outreach. It rarely requires looking at anything uncomfortable close to home.

Pouring more leads into a conversion process that's already broken just produces more people falling through the same cracks, faster and more expensively.

The founders who actually fix growth start with a harder question: why aren't the leads we already have converting? That answer is usually less flattering and far more useful than "we need more volume."
