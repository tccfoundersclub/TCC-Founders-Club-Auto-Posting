Perfectionism feels like a commitment to quality. Often it's just energy pointed at the easiest thing to polish - the design, the copy, the deck - instead of the part of the idea that's actually uncertain.

The riskiest, least-proven assumption in most new ideas rarely gets the most attention before launch, because it's harder and scarier to test directly. It's much more comfortable to keep refining the parts you already know work.

The founders who move fastest identify the one real uncertainty in the idea and go test that specifically, leaving everything else deliberately rough until it's earned the polish.
