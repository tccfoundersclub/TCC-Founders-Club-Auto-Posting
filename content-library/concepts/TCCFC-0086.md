TOPIC:
A founder's actual calendar reveals real priorities, not their stated ones

HOOK:
Your Calendar Is Telling the Truth About Your Priorities, Even When You Aren't

INTRO:
Founders say one thing matters most. Point 2 is the gap that usually shows up.

POINT 1:
A founder can say "customers are our top priority" while their calendar shows almost no time actually talking to one.

POINT 2:
The calendar doesn't lie the way the stated priority list can - it shows exactly where the hours actually went.

POINT 3:
The founders who close that gap audit their own calendar honestly, on a regular basis, against what they claim matters most.

CAPTION:
Ask a founder what matters most and you'll usually get a clean, confident answer - customers, the product, the team. Look at their actual calendar from the last month and the story often doesn't match.

A calendar full of internal meetings and almost no direct customer time tells a different truth than the stated priority does. The calendar doesn't perform for anyone. It just shows exactly where the hours actually went.

The founders who close that gap do something uncomfortable but simple: they audit their own calendar honestly against what they claim matters most, on a regular basis, and adjust when the two don't line up.
