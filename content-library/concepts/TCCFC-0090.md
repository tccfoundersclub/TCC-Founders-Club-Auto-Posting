The Co-Founder Resentment That Started With Nobody Ever Defining Who Owns What

CAPTION:
Co-founder resentment rarely starts with one big blowup. It usually starts small: unclear ownership means both people occasionally step on each other's decisions, or a gap opens up where both assumed the other had it covered.

Neither of those feels like a big deal in the moment. Repeated enough times, they compound into a quiet resentment that's hard to trace back to any single event, which makes it even harder to address directly.

The founder pairs who avoid this defined clear ownership early - who owns what, who has final say where - even when it felt like an unnecessary formality at the time. It wasn't.
