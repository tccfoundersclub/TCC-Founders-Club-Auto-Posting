TOPIC:
Proximity to money changes how founders treat old friends

HOOK:
Money Changes How People Treat You. What Changes You Is How You Handle That.

INTRO:
Founders notice friends acting differently after success. Point 2 is the part they miss.

POINT 1:
Some friends do pull back or act differently once a founder has visible success - that part is often real and worth noticing.

POINT 2:
What's less noticed is how a founder's own behavior can shift first, without realizing it, once money enters the picture.

POINT 3:
The founders who keep old friendships intact stay deliberately aware of their own side of that shift, not just watching for it in others.

CAPTION:
Founders often notice, sometimes painfully, that old friends start acting a little differently once real success shows up - more distant, more transactional, or overly deferential in a way that feels strange. That part is real and worth taking seriously.

What gets noticed less is the founder's own side of it: shifts in who gets called back quickly, who gets the benefit of the doubt, how casually money gets discussed around people who don't have it. That change is easy to miss from the inside.

The founders who keep their old friendships genuinely intact stay deliberately aware of their own behavior shifting, not just watching for it in everyone else.
