TOPIC:
An off-limits, zero-work-talk day protects founder friendships

HOOK:
The Founder Friendships That Last Have One Rule: Sometimes, No Work Talk

INTRO:
Every hangout defaults to shop talk. Point 2 is what that habit slowly costs.

POINT 1:
Founder friendships often exist entirely inside work conversation, which makes them feel productive and quietly one-dimensional.

POINT 2:
Without ever turning work off, the friendship never gets tested or nourished as anything other than a professional resource.

POINT 3:
The founders whose friendships last longest protect deliberate time with zero business talk allowed, on purpose.

CAPTION:
Two founders hang out and the conversation drifts to work within the first ten minutes, almost every time. It feels natural - it's what's top of mind for both people, and it feels productive to talk shop.

Left unchecked, that pattern means the friendship exists entirely inside a work frame. It never really gets tested or nourished as anything other than a useful professional resource, which is a thinner kind of relationship than it looks like from the outside.

The founder friendships that genuinely last have usually built in the opposite habit on purpose - time together with an explicit no-work-talk rule, protecting the part of the relationship that isn't about the business at all.
