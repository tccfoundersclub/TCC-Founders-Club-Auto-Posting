TOPIC:
A founder's "no" list matters as much as their "yes" list

HOOK:
Your List of Things You'll Never Do Matters as Much as Your List of Goals

INTRO:
Founders obsess over what they're building toward. Point 2 is the equally useful opposite.

POINT 1:
A clear list of goals tells you what to move toward. It doesn't protect you from drifting into things that quietly don't fit.

POINT 2:
A "no" list - specific things you've decided never to do, regardless of opportunity - makes fast decisions easier under pressure.

POINT 3:
The founders who stay coherent as they grow usually have both lists, and refer to the "no" list just as often as the goals.

CAPTION:
Founders are good at articulating goals - where the company is headed, what they're building toward. Far fewer have an equally clear list of the opposite: specific things they've decided never to do, no matter how good the opportunity looks in the moment.

A goals list tells you what to move toward. It doesn't protect you from drift - the slow accumulation of decisions that each seemed fine individually but pulled you somewhere you didn't actually want to go.

A clear "no" list makes fast decisions easier under pressure, because the hard thinking already happened, calmly, in advance. The founders who stay coherent as they scale usually have both lists, and use the "no" list just as often.
