TOPIC:
Silence from a customer is often worse news than a complaint

HOOK:
A Silent Customer Is Worse News Than an Angry One

INTRO:
Founders dread complaints. Point 2 is the signal that should actually worry them more.

POINT 1:
A complaint means a customer still cares enough to tell you something's wrong - that's still an open door.

POINT 2:
Silence usually means they've quietly checked out and are already looking elsewhere, without bothering to explain why.

POINT 3:
The founders who catch churn early watch for silence, not just complaints, because silence is the harder signal to notice.

CAPTION:
Founders tend to dread the angry email, the harsh review, the frustrated call. Those are uncomfortable, and they're also a good sign in a strange way - the customer still cares enough to tell you something's wrong. That's a door still open.

Silence is the worse signal. A customer who's gone quiet - fewer logins, no replies, no complaints, nothing - has usually already checked out mentally and started looking elsewhere. They didn't think it was worth the effort to explain why.

The founders who catch churn early are watching for silence, not just complaints, because silence is by far the harder signal to notice in time.
