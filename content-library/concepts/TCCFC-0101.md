A founder's monthly personal reflection matters as much as the business update

HOOK:
You Write a Monthly Business Update. When's the Last Time You Wrote One About Yourself?

INTRO:
Founders track the company religiously. Point 2 is what rarely gets the same treatment.

POINT 1:
Business metrics get reviewed monthly, sometimes weekly - real discipline around tracking progress.

POINT 2:
The founder's own state - energy, stress, satisfaction, direction - rarely gets that same structured attention.

POINT 3:
The founders who stay steady over years have usually built some version of a personal check-in, not just a business one.

CAPTION:
Most founders have real discipline around business metrics - a monthly review, a dashboard, a clear read on how things are trending. Ask when they last did anything similar for themselves, and the answer is usually "I don't really do that."

The business gets tracked. The founder's own state - energy, stress, whether the work still feels meaningful - runs entirely on vibes, checked only when something feels badly enough off to notice.

The founders who stay steady over the years have usually built some version of a personal check-in too - not as elaborate as the business review, but real, regular, and honest.
