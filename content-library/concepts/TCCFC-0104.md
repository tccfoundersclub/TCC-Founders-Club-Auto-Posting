TOPIC:
Only networking with peers at your exact stage creates its own echo chamber

HOOK:
Only Talking to Founders at Your Exact Stage Has a Blind Spot Too

INTRO:
It feels like the most relevant peer group. Point 2 is what it quietly limits.

POINT 1:
Peers at your exact stage share your current problems, but also your current blind spots - nobody in the room has already solved what you haven't.

POINT 2:
A room of only same-stage founders can accidentally reinforce shared assumptions instead of challenging them.

POINT 3:
The founders who see furthest mix same-stage peers with people a little ahead and a little behind, on purpose.

CAPTION:
A peer group made entirely of founders at your exact stage feels like the most relevant room you could be in - everyone's dealing with similar problems, similar pressures, similar conversations. That similarity is also a limitation.

Same-stage peers share your current blind spots as much as your current problems. Nobody in a room like that has already solved what none of you have solved yet, which means the group can accidentally reinforce shared assumptions instead of challenging them.

The founders who see furthest deliberately mix in people a little ahead and a little behind their own stage, not just people who mirror it.

DM "TRIBE" for a room that spans stages, not just matches yours.
