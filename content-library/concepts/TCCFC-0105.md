TOPIC:
Hiring for culture fit only, while ignoring real skill gaps, backfires

HOOK:
"Great Culture Fit" Isn't Enough If They Can't Actually Do the Job

INTRO:
Culture fit feels like the safer hiring bar. Point 2 is what it can quietly excuse.

POINT 1:
A candidate who's easy to talk to and clearly aligned with the team's values can still be genuinely unqualified for the role.

POINT 2:
Founders sometimes let a strong culture-fit feeling override real doubts about whether someone can actually execute.

POINT 3:
The founders who build strong teams treat culture fit as a real filter and skill as a completely separate, equally non-negotiable one.

CAPTION:
"Great culture fit" has become one of the most common reasons founders give for a hire that later doesn't work out. The person was likeable, aligned with the team's values, easy to imagine working with - and somewhere in that warm feeling, real doubts about whether they could actually do the job got quietly waved off.

Culture fit is a real and important filter. It's not a substitute for competence, and treating it like one is how teams end up with people everyone likes and nobody can fully rely on.

The founders who build strong teams treat the two as separate, equally non-negotiable filters - not one standing in for the other.
