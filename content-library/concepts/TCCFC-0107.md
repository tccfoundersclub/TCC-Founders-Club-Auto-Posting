TOPIC:
The difference between a coach and a mentor is process, and founders undervalue coaching

HOOK:
A Mentor Gives You Advice. A Coach Gives You a Process. You Probably Need Both.

INTRO:
Founders default to seeking mentors. Point 2 is the different value a coach provides.

POINT 1:
A mentor draws on their own specific experience to tell you what they'd do - valuable, and shaped by one person's path.

POINT 2:
A coach doesn't tell you what to do - they build a structured process that helps you find your own answer, repeatably.

POINT 3:
The founders who grow fastest usually have both - a mentor for perspective, a coach for the discipline of consistently thinking well.

CAPTION:
Founders naturally seek out mentors - people further along who can say "here's what I'd do in your situation." That's genuinely useful, and it's also shaped by one specific person's specific path, which may or may not map cleanly onto yours.

A coach works differently. They're not handing you an answer from their own experience - they're building a structured process that helps you find your own answer, and helps you find it again next time, without needing them in the room.

The founders who grow fastest often have both: a mentor for perspective and pattern-matching, a coach for the discipline of consistently thinking clearly under pressure.
