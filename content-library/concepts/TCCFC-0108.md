TOPIC:
Founders build without ever pressure-testing ideas with a genuine skeptic

HOOK:
You Need One Person in Your Life Whose Job Is to Tell You Why It Won't Work

INTRO:
Founders surround themselves with encouragement. Point 2 is what that comfort can cost.

POINT 1:
A room full of supportive voices feels great and rarely catches the real flaw in an idea before launch.

POINT 2:
A genuine skeptic - someone who'll actually argue the case against your plan - surfaces problems encouragement never will.

POINT 3:
The founders who build resilient ideas deliberately seek out that skeptical voice instead of only surrounding themselves with belief.

CAPTION:
It feels good to be surrounded by people who believe in the idea - encouraging, supportive, rooting for you. It also means almost nobody in the room is doing the uncomfortable work of arguing the case against your plan before you've spent months building it.

A genuine skeptic - someone willing to push back hard, not just to be difficult, but because they actually think it through - surfaces problems that a room full of encouragement never will.

The founders who build the most resilient ideas seek that voice out on purpose, even though it's less pleasant than the alternative. Better to hear it now than after the launch.
