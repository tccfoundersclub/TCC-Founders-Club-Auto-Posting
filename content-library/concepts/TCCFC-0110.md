TOPIC:
Founders never renegotiate vendor contracts, quietly overpaying for years

HOOK:
You're Probably Still Paying the Rate You Negotiated Two Years Ago, and Nobody Checked

INTRO:
Vendor contracts get set once and forgotten. Point 2 is what that costs quietly.

POINT 1:
A rate negotiated once, early, often just keeps renewing without anyone revisiting whether it's still competitive.

POINT 2:
Vendors rarely offer a better rate unprompted - the renegotiation has to come from you, and almost nobody schedules it.

POINT 3:
The founders who protect margin do a regular vendor audit, treating old contracts as worth revisiting, not fixed forever.

CAPTION:
A vendor rate negotiated early, in the first year, often just auto-renews year after year without anyone ever circling back to check if it's still fair. Nobody's being deliberately overcharged - it's just that renegotiating isn't anyone's job unless someone makes it their job.

Vendors almost never offer a better rate unprompted. The ask has to come from you, and it's an easy thing to never quite get around to, especially when the amount doesn't feel urgent enough to prioritize.

The founders who protect their margins do a regular audit of standing vendor contracts, and treat "we've always paid this" as a reason to check, not a reason to leave it alone.
