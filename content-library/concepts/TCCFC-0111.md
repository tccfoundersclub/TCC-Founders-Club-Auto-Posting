TOPIC:
Not having a crisis communication plan before a crisis hits costs founders time when it matters most

HOOK:
The Worst Time to Figure Out How You'll Communicate a Crisis Is During One

INTRO:
Founders plan for growth scenarios. Point 2 is the plan almost nobody makes.

POINT 1:
Most founders have thought through how they'd communicate a big win. Very few have thought through how they'd communicate a real crisis.

POINT 2:
Figuring out what to say, to whom, and in what order takes real time - time you don't have once the crisis is already unfolding.

POINT 3:
The founders who handle crises with composure usually have a rough plan already in mind, built calmly, well before anything went wrong.

CAPTION:
Founders spend real time imagining how they'd announce a big win - the raise, the launch, the milestone. Almost nobody spends the same time imagining how they'd communicate a real crisis: a data issue, a public complaint, a sudden layoff.

Figuring out what to say, to whom, in what order, takes real thought - and during an actual crisis, that thinking time is exactly what you don't have. Decisions get made fast, under pressure, often worse than they would have been with a calm head.

The founders who handle hard moments with composure usually have a rough communication plan already sketched out, built during a calm moment, long before they needed it.
