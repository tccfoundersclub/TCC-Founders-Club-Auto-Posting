TOPIC:
Regular customer discovery calls should continue even post-PMF

HOOK:
You Stopped Doing Customer Discovery Calls Right When You Should've Kept Going

INTRO:
Founders treat discovery calls as a pre-PMF phase. Point 2 is why that's a mistake.

POINT 1:
Discovery calls feel like something you do before you've found product-market fit, then quietly stop once things are working.

POINT 2:
A market that fit well a year ago can shift without the founder noticing, because nobody's actively listening for it anymore.

POINT 3:
The founders who stay ahead of market shifts keep a regular cadence of real customer conversations, long after they'd have any obvious reason to.

CAPTION:
Customer discovery calls get treated like a pre-product-market-fit activity - necessary early on, then quietly dropped once the business is working and there's less obvious pressure to keep having them.

A market that fit well a year ago doesn't necessarily stay that way, and a founder who's stopped actively listening won't notice the shift until it shows up somewhere expensive, like slowing growth or rising churn.

The founders who stay ahead of market changes keep a regular cadence of real customer conversations going, long after there's an obvious reason to - because the value of those calls isn't just finding fit, it's noticing when fit starts to change.
