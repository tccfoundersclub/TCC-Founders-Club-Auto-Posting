TOPIC:
Founder friendships need deliberate protection through the stress of a fundraise

HOOK:
A Fundraise Is Hard on the Business and Quietly Harder on Founder Friendships Than People Expect

INTRO:
The stress gets absorbed by the business. Point 2 is where it also leaks.

POINT 1:
The stress of a raise doesn't stay contained to work conversations - it shows up shorter, more distracted, less present with friends too.

POINT 2:
Founder friendships that don't get deliberately protected during a raise sometimes go quiet for months without either side meaning for that to happen.

POINT 3:
The founders who keep their friendships intact through a raise name the stress out loud to their friends, instead of just disappearing into it.

CAPTION:
A fundraise is hard on the business in ways everyone expects - the pitches, the rejections, the waiting. It's also quietly hard on founder friendships in a way people don't talk about as much: less bandwidth, more distraction, less presence in conversations that used to feel easy.

Without anyone meaning for it to happen, a friendship can go quiet for months during a raise, simply because there was nothing left over some weeks to give it.

The founders who keep those friendships intact through the stress name it out loud - "I'm heads-down on a raise, I might be less present, that's not about you" - instead of just disappearing and letting the friend guess why.
