TOPIC:
Over-indexing on a single big customer creates dangerous concentration risk

HOOK:
Your Biggest Customer Is Also Your Biggest Risk

INTRO:
A whale customer feels like a win. Point 2 is the danger hiding inside it.

POINT 1:
Landing one customer that makes up a huge share of revenue feels like validation and quietly becomes a single point of failure.

POINT 2:
That customer's priorities start shaping the roadmap, sometimes at the expense of everyone else who might use the product.

POINT 3:
The founders who avoid this trap actively diversify even while the big customer feels great, instead of waiting until losing them forces the issue.

CAPTION:
Landing a big customer whose revenue dwarfs everyone else's feels like validation - proof the product matters, a number worth celebrating. It's also, quietly, a single point of failure sitting under the whole business.

That customer's specific needs start shaping the roadmap, sometimes at the expense of the broader market the product is supposed to serve. And if they ever leave, the hole they leave behind is far bigger than any one customer should be able to create.

The founders who avoid this trap diversify deliberately while the big customer still feels great - not after losing them forces the conversation.
