TOPIC:
Comparing your fundraise timeline to someone else's is misleading

HOOK:
Someone Else's 3-Month Raise Doesn't Mean Yours Should Take 3 Months Too

INTRO:
Fundraise timelines get compared like a fair benchmark. Point 2 is why they rarely are one.

POINT 1:
A fast raise usually reflects a specific market moment, a specific network, or a specific story - not a universal timeline everyone should hit.

POINT 2:
Comparing your own longer, harder raise to someone else's fast one adds pressure and stress without adding any useful information.

POINT 3:
The founders who stay grounded through a hard raise measure their own progress against their own market and story, not someone else's timeline.

CAPTION:
A peer closes a round in three months and it's easy to quietly measure your own six-month slog against that as if it's a fair comparison. It rarely is - a fast raise usually reflects a specific market window, a specific founder network, or a specific story that happened to land, none of which are universal.

Comparing timelines like that adds real pressure and stress without adding any useful information about your own situation.

The founders who stay grounded through a harder, slower raise measure their own progress against their own market and story - not against a timeline that was shaped by circumstances they don't share.
