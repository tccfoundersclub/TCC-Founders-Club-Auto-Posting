TOPIC:
"We'll fix it in v2" becomes a permanent excuse

HOOK:
"We'll Fix It in V2" Has a Way of Never Actually Happening

INTRO:
It sounds like a reasonable deferral. Point 2 is why it rarely gets revisited.

POINT 1:
Deferring a known problem to "v2" feels responsible in the moment - ship now, improve later.

POINT 2:
Once v1 ships, attention moves to whatever's next, and the deferred fix quietly competes with new priorities it usually loses to.

POINT 3:
The founders who actually ship real improvements put a specific date or trigger on the "later" fix, instead of leaving it as a vague future intention.

CAPTION:
"We'll fix it in v2" sounds like a responsible tradeoff - ship what's working now, come back to the rough edges later. In practice, "later" competes against whatever's newly urgent once v1 is out, and it usually loses.

The deferred fix doesn't disappear. It just sits there, quietly known and quietly ignored, until a customer complaint or a competitor forces the issue months later than it needed to.

The founders who actually follow through attach something specific to the deferral - a date, a trigger, a real commitment - instead of leaving "v2" as a vague, unscheduled someday.
