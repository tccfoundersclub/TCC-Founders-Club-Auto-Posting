TOPIC:
Teams stop trusting a roadmap that changes too often without explanation

HOOK:
Your Roadmap Keeps Changing and Nobody's Telling the Team Why

INTRO:
Founders adjust plans as they learn. Point 2 is what happens when the reasoning stays invisible.

POINT 1:
Adjusting the roadmap as new information comes in is healthy and normal - what damages trust is doing it without explaining why.

POINT 2:
A team that sees priorities shift with no visible reasoning starts assuming the plan was never solid in the first place.

POINT 3:
The founders who keep team trust through frequent roadmap changes explain the "why" every time, even briefly, not just the "what."

CAPTION:
Adjusting the roadmap as new information comes in is exactly what a healthy, responsive company does. What damages trust isn't the change itself - it's changing direction without ever explaining why, leaving the team to guess.

A team that watches priorities shift repeatedly with no visible reasoning starts to assume the whole plan was never that solid to begin with, and starts treating every roadmap update as provisional, worth less than full commitment.

The founders who keep team trust intact through frequent, healthy changes explain the "why" every time, even briefly - not just announcing the new "what" and moving on.
