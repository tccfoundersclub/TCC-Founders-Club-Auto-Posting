TOPIC:
Founders lack a clear personal financial runway plan, separate from the company's

HOOK:
You Have a Runway Number for the Company. Do You Have One for Yourself?

INTRO:
Founders track company runway obsessively. Point 2 is the equally important number they skip.

POINT 1:
Company runway gets tracked to the month, sometimes the week. Personal financial runway - how long the founder can sustain themselves - often isn't tracked at all.

POINT 2:
Without that number, personal financial stress becomes background noise that quietly affects decisions without ever being named directly.

POINT 3:
The founders who make clearer decisions know both numbers - the company's and their own - and don't let one hide the other.

CAPTION:
Company runway gets watched closely - the number founders can usually rattle off without hesitation. Personal financial runway - how long the founder themselves can sustain a low or no salary - often isn't tracked with anywhere near the same rigor, sometimes not at all.

Without that number, personal financial stress becomes quiet background noise, influencing decisions - what risks feel acceptable, how urgently a raise feels necessary - without ever being named directly or factored in deliberately.

The founders who make clearer decisions know both numbers, the company's and their own, and don't let personal financial pressure operate invisibly underneath every other call they make.
