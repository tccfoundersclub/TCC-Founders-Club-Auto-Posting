TOPIC:
"Culture eats strategy" gets misapplied to avoid making hard strategic calls

HOOK:
"Culture Eats Strategy" Gets Used as an Excuse to Avoid Making the Hard Strategic Call

INTRO:
The phrase gets quoted often. Point 2 is how it sometimes gets misused.

POINT 1:
The phrase is genuinely true in the sense that culture undermines bad execution of good strategy - it's often misused to mean strategy doesn't matter much at all.

POINT 2:
Founders sometimes hide behind "we just need the right culture" instead of doing the harder work of a clear, specific strategic decision.

POINT 3:
The founders who build durable companies do both - real strategic clarity and a culture that can actually execute it - instead of leaning on one to excuse skipping the other.

CAPTION:
"Culture eats strategy for breakfast" gets quoted constantly, and it's genuinely true in its original sense - a bad culture will sabotage even a brilliant strategy. It also sometimes gets misused to mean strategy barely matters at all, which is a much weaker and more convenient claim.

That misuse gives founders cover to avoid the harder work of an actual, specific strategic decision - "we'll figure it out, we just need the right culture and people" - when what's actually missing is real clarity about direction.

The founders who build durable companies do both: real strategic clarity, and a culture capable of executing it well. Neither one is a substitute for the other, no matter how the quote gets used.
