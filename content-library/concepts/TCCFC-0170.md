TOPIC:
Founders ignore early signs of team burnout until it's a crisis

HOOK:
The Signs of Team Burnout Show Up Long Before Anyone Calls It Burnout

INTRO:
Burnout gets addressed once it's already severe. Point 2 is what gets missed earlier.

POINT 1:
Small early signs - shorter replies, less initiative, quieter meetings - often get read as a busy week, not the early stage of burnout.

POINT 2:
By the time burnout is undeniable, it's usually been building quietly for months, unaddressed because nobody named it early.

POINT 3:
The founders who catch burnout early watch for those small shifts specifically, and check in directly instead of waiting for an obvious crisis.

CAPTION:
Burnout rarely announces itself clearly. It shows up first as small, easy-to-explain-away shifts - shorter replies, less initiative on things that used to get volunteered for, quieter contributions in meetings - and gets read as "a busy week" rather than an early warning sign.

By the time burnout is undeniable - someone's clearly checked out, or actually breaks down, or quits - it's usually been building quietly for months, unaddressed because nobody named the early signals when they first appeared.

The founders who catch this early are watching for those small shifts specifically, and check in directly and genuinely, rather than waiting for a crisis that's obvious enough to force the conversation.
