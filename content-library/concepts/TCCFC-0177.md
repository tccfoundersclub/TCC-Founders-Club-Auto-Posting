TOPIC:
Waiting for the "right" logo/branding before launching delays real learning

HOOK:
Waiting for the Perfect Logo Before You Launch Is Costing You Real Customer Feedback

INTRO:
Branding feels like a prerequisite. Point 2 is why that sequencing is usually backwards.

POINT 1:
A polished brand identity feels like a prerequisite for launching, and it's rarely what determines whether the first version of the idea actually works.

POINT 2:
Time spent perfecting branding before any real customer feedback exists is time spent optimizing based on guesses, not data.

POINT 3:
The founders who learn fastest launch with a rough, honest version first, and let branding evolve once there's real signal to build it around.

CAPTION:
A polished logo and brand identity can feel like a prerequisite before anything goes live - like launching without it would look unprofessional or unfinished. In practice, branding is rarely what determines whether the underlying idea actually works for real customers.

Time spent perfecting visual identity before any real customer feedback exists is time spent optimizing based on guesses about what matters, rather than actual data about what customers respond to.

The founders who learn fastest launch with something rough and honest first, and let the branding evolve once there's real signal - actual usage, actual feedback - to build it around.
