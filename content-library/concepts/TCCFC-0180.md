TOPIC:
A founder who never says thank you specifically, only generally, weakens its impact

HOOK:
A General "Thanks Everyone" Doesn't Land the Way a Specific Thank You Does

INTRO:
Founders default to broad, group-level gratitude. Point 2 is why it lands weaker than intended.

POINT 1:
"Thanks everyone, great work" is easy to say and easy to not really hear - nobody feels personally seen by it.

POINT 2:
A specific thank you - naming exactly what someone did and why it mattered - lands completely differently and is remembered much longer.

POINT 3:
The founders who build real loyalty make specific gratitude a habit, even though it takes more effort than a general shoutout.

CAPTION:
"Thanks everyone, great work this week" is easy to say in a team meeting, and it's easy to not really hear too - a general compliment to a group rarely makes any one person feel genuinely seen.

A specific thank you lands completely differently: naming exactly what someone did, why it mattered, what it made possible. That kind of specificity is remembered for months. The general version is usually forgotten by the next day.

The founders who build real loyalty make specific gratitude a habit, even though it takes noticeably more effort and attention than a broad, easy shoutout to the whole team.
