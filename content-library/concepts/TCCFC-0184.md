TOPIC:
Founders should track their own energy patterns, not just their time

HOOK:
You Track Your Time Religiously. Do You Track When You Actually Have Energy?

INTRO:
Time management gets real attention. Point 2 is the equally important thing that doesn't.

POINT 1:
Time blocked on a calendar says nothing about whether that block lands during a founder's naturally sharp hours or their naturally depleted ones.

POINT 2:
Scheduling the hardest thinking work during a low-energy window wastes both the time and the energy that would've matched better elsewhere.

POINT 3:
The founders who get more out of the same number of hours track their own energy patterns and match the hardest work to their actual peak windows.

CAPTION:
Time management gets real discipline from most founders - calendars, blocks, careful scheduling. Energy management gets almost none, even though a scheduled hour during a naturally sharp window produces completely different output than the same hour during a depleted one.

Putting the hardest strategic thinking into a low-energy afternoon slot, just because that's when the calendar had room, wastes both the time and the energy that would have matched much better with a different task at a different hour.

The founders who get more out of the same number of hours actually notice their own energy patterns and deliberately match the hardest work to their real peak windows, not just whatever slot happened to be free.
