CAPTION:
A strong candidate who turned down an offer a year or two ago did so for specific reasons at that specific moment - a different job felt more exciting, the timing wasn't right, the risk didn't feel worth it yet. None of that has to still be true now.

Most founders treat that old "no" as permanent and never circle back, when the honest answer is usually just that nobody checked whether the circumstances had changed.

The founders who build the strongest teams keep a quiet list of people who almost joined, and reach back out periodically and respectfully, because sometimes the timing that was wrong before is exactly right now.
