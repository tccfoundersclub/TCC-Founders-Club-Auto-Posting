TOPIC:
Founders should treat their calendar as a communication tool for the team

HOOK:
Your Calendar Is Communicating Something to Your Team Whether You Mean It to or Not

INTRO:
Founders think of their calendar as personal logistics. Point 2 is why the team reads it as more than that.

POINT 1:
A team can see meeting titles, availability, and patterns in a founder's calendar, and they read meaning into all of it, intentionally or not.

POINT 2:
A calendar that's mysterious or constantly shifting creates uncertainty about what the founder is actually prioritizing.

POINT 3:
The founders who communicate well treat calendar transparency as part of leadership communication, not just personal scheduling.

CAPTION:
A founder's calendar looks like pure personal logistics from the inside and reads as information to everyone else - meeting titles, patterns of availability, what gets prioritized over what. The team notices, and draws conclusions, whether or not that was the intent.

A calendar that's mysterious, constantly shifting, or full of vague blocks creates quiet uncertainty about what's actually being prioritized at the top, even when nothing is actually being hidden.

The founders who communicate well treat some level of calendar transparency as part of leadership communication itself, not just personal scheduling - giving the team a clearer, more accurate read on where attention is actually going.
