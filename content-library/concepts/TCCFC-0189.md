Founders confuse politeness with honesty in feedback

HOOK:
Being Polite in Feedback Isn't the Same as Being Honest, and the Difference Costs You

INTRO:
Softening feedback feels kind. Point 2 is what gets lost in the softening.

POINT 1:
Wrapping feedback in enough cushioning to feel gentle sometimes strips out the actual point, leaving the person unsure what needs to change.

POINT 2:

CAPTION:
Softening feedback with enough cushioning to feel gentle is a natural instinct, and it can go far enough that the actual point gets lost entirely - the person walks away polite and confused, without a clear sense of what actually needs to change.

That kind of politeness isn't really kindness. It just delays a harder, more confusing version of the same conversation for later, once the pattern has repeated enough times that it can't be softened anymore.

The founders who give genuinely useful feedback are warm in how they deliver it and completely clear in the substance - the two aren't actually in tension, even though it can feel that way in the moment.
