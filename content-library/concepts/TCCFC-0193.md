TOPIC:
Founders lack a written definition of "done" for launches

HOOK:
What Does "Done" Actually Mean for Your Launch? Most Teams Have Never Written It Down.

INTRO:
Launch scope creeps without a clear finish line. Point 2 is why that costs momentum.

POINT 1:
Without a written definition of done, "just one more thing" quietly extends a launch date again and again.

POINT 2:
Ambiguity about what's actually required lets perfectionism and scope creep both hide behind "we're just being thorough."

POINT 3:
The founders who ship on time write down a specific, minimal definition of done before starting, and hold the team to exactly that.

CAPTION:
Ask a team "what does done actually mean for this launch?" and the answer is often surprisingly vague - a general sense rather than a specific, written list. That vagueness is exactly where launch dates quietly slip.

Without a clear line, "just one more thing before we ship" keeps finding new things to add, and both perfectionism and genuine scope creep get to hide behind the same reasonable-sounding phrase: we're just being thorough.

The founders who ship reliably on time write down a specific, deliberately minimal definition of done before starting, and hold the team to exactly that line, resisting the pull to keep adding right up until launch.
