TOPIC:
Founders never ask customers what almost stopped them from buying

HOOK:
What Almost Stopped Your Customer From Buying? You've Probably Never Asked.

INTRO:
Founders ask why customers bought. Point 2 is the equally revealing question they skip.

POINT 1:
"Why did you buy?" gets asked regularly and gives a clean, positive story about the decision.

POINT 2:
"What almost stopped you?" surfaces the real friction and hesitation - pricing doubt, unclear messaging, a competitor they nearly chose instead.

POINT 3:
The founders who remove real barriers to purchase ask this second question deliberately, because it reveals exactly what to fix.

CAPTION:
"Why did you decide to buy?" is a question a lot of founders ask, and it produces a clean, positive story - the reasons that worked, told after the decision was already made and justified to themselves.

"What almost stopped you?" is a completely different, rarer question, and it surfaces the real friction: the pricing that gave them pause, the messaging that was confusing, the competitor they almost chose instead. That's the information that actually tells you what to fix.

The founders who systematically remove barriers to purchase ask this second question deliberately and often, because it reveals exactly what nearly cost them the sale - information the first question never surfaces.
