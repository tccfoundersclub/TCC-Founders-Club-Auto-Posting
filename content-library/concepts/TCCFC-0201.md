TOPIC:
Building relationships with local press before needing them pays off later

HOOK:
The Time to Build a Relationship With a Journalist Is Before You Need Coverage, Not During a Launch

INTRO:
Founders reach out to press only when they need something. Point 2 is why that timing weakens the ask.

POINT 1:
Cold-emailing a journalist only when there's a launch to cover reads as transactional, and it usually is.

POINT 2:
A relationship built earlier - genuinely helpful context, no immediate ask attached - makes the eventual pitch land completely differently.

POINT 3:
The founders who get consistent, fair coverage over time built the relationship before they needed anything specific from it.

CAPTION:
Reaching out to a journalist for the first time exactly when there's a launch to cover is a common pattern, and it reads as exactly what it is - transactional, need-based, easy to deprioritize among a dozen similar pitches landing the same week.

A relationship built earlier - offering genuinely useful context on a story with no immediate ask attached, engaging with their work without expecting anything back - changes how the eventual pitch lands completely, because there's already a real relationship behind it.

The founders who get consistent, fair press coverage over time built those relationships before they needed anything specific, treating journalists as people worth knowing, not just a channel to activate on demand.
