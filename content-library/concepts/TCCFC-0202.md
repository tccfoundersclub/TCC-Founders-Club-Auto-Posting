TOPIC:
Founders never revisit pricing tiers after initial launch

HOOK:
Your Pricing Tiers Were Set at Launch and Probably Haven't Been Touched Since

INTRO:
Initial pricing structure often just persists. Point 2 is why that's worth revisiting.

POINT 1:
Pricing tiers set at launch reflected a guess about the market, made with the least amount of real customer data the company will ever have.

POINT 2:
Left unexamined, that early guess just becomes permanent structure, even as real usage data reveals what customers actually value and pay for.

POINT 3:
The founders who optimize revenue revisit pricing tiers periodically with real data, instead of treating the launch-day guess as fixed forever.

CAPTION:
Pricing tiers set at launch were a genuine best guess, made with the least amount of real customer data the company will ever have access to - no usage patterns yet, no sense of what people actually value enough to pay more for.

Left unexamined, that early guess just quietly becomes permanent structure. Months or years of real usage data accumulate, showing exactly what customers value and where the tiers don't quite match real behavior, and none of it gets incorporated because the original structure was never revisited.

The founders who optimize revenue well revisit pricing tiers periodically, using real data instead of the original launch-day guess, and aren't precious about a structure that was never meant to be permanent.
