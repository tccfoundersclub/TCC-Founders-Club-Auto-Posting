CAPTION:
Waiting for a mentor to spontaneously think to introduce you to someone useful in their network leaves a genuinely valuable connection entirely up to chance - it might occur to them, and it might just never come up.

A specific, respectful, direct ask - "would you be open to introducing me to X, I think there could be real value on both sides" - is far more likely to actually happen than hoping it occurs to someone unprompted, no matter how good the relationship is.

The founders who build the strongest networks ask directly and specifically, and make the ask genuinely easy to say yes or no to, instead of leaving valuable connections to chance.
