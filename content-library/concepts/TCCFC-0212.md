TOPIC:
Founders treat "no news is good news" from a team member as literal truth

HOOK:
"No News Is Good News" From Your Team Is Sometimes Just Silence You Haven't Investigated

INTRO:
A quiet team member gets assumed to be fine. Point 2 is why that assumption can mislead.

POINT 1:
A team member who's gone quiet - fewer updates, less initiative - sometimes genuinely is fine, and sometimes is quietly struggling or checked out.

POINT 2:
Without actually checking, a founder can't actually tell the difference, and defaults to assuming the more convenient explanation.

POINT 3:
The founders who catch problems early check in directly with quiet team members instead of assuming silence means everything's fine.

CAPTION:
A team member who's gone quiet lately - fewer updates, less initiative, shorter answers - can genuinely be fine, just heads-down and focused. They can also be quietly struggling, disengaged, or already halfway out the door. From the outside, both look identical: silence.

Without actually checking in directly, there's no way to tell the difference, and it's easy to default to the more convenient explanation - they're probably just busy - simply because it requires nothing further.

The founders who catch real problems early check in directly and specifically with quiet team members, instead of assuming silence automatically means everything is fine.
