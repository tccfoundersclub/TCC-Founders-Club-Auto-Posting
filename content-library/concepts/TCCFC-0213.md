TOPIC:
Founders never ask "what would make you quit" to key people

HOOK:
Have You Ever Actually Asked Your Best People What Would Make Them Leave?

INTRO:
Founders assume they'd know if a key person were flight-risk. Point 2 is why that assumption is shaky.

POINT 1:
Most founders have never asked their best people directly what circumstances would make them consider leaving.

POINT 2:
Without asking, retention risk stays invisible until someone's already decided and is handing in notice.

POINT 3:
The founders who retain key people proactively ask this question directly, periodically, and actually act on the answers.

CAPTION:
Most founders have never directly asked their best people the specific question: what would make you consider leaving? It feels like an uncomfortable, almost provocative thing to ask, so it just never comes up.

Without asking, retention risk for key people stays completely invisible - right up until someone's already made the decision internally and is handing in their notice, by which point there's usually very little left to do about it.

The founders who retain their best people proactively ask this question directly and periodically, and take the answers seriously enough to actually act on them before it's too late.
