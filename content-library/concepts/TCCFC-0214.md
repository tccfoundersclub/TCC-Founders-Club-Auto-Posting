A massive total addressable market number looks great on a slide, and it says almost nothing about how hard it'll actually be to capture any meaningful share of it. Big and easy are not the same thing - they're often the opposite.

Larger markets frequently come with more entrenched, well-funded competitors, higher costs to acquire attention and customers, and more noise to cut through just to be noticed at all.

The founders who actually succeed in large markets have a specific, defensible wedge - a starting point they can genuinely win - not just a big TAM number they're pointing to as if size alone were a strategy.
