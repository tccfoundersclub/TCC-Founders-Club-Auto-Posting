TOPIC:
Founders over-rely on their own intuition about UX without real user testing

HOOK:
Your Gut Feeling About the UX Isn't a Substitute for Watching a Real User Struggle With It

INTRO:
Founders trust their own product instincts. Point 2 is the blind spot that creates.

POINT 1:
A founder who's used the product hundreds of times has instincts a first-time user simply doesn't share.

POINT 2:
What feels obvious and intuitive to the founder can be genuinely confusing to someone encountering it fresh, and the founder can't feel that gap from the inside.

POINT 3:
The founders who ship genuinely usable products watch real, unscripted user sessions regularly, instead of relying solely on their own sense of what feels right.

CAPTION:
A founder who's used their own product hundreds of times has deep, genuine instincts about how it should work - instincts a first-time user simply doesn't have and can't be expected to share, no matter how intuitive it feels from the inside.

What feels completely obvious to someone who built the thing can be genuinely confusing to someone encountering it fresh, and there's no way to feel that gap from the founder's own seat - it has to be observed directly.

The founders who ship genuinely usable products watch real, unscripted user sessions regularly, and treat their own gut feeling about the UX as a hypothesis to test, not a verdict to trust on its own.
