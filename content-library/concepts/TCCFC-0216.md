You'd Negotiate Hard for Your Team's Salaries. Do You Negotiate for Your Own?

INTRO:
Founders often underpay themselves without examination. Point 2 is why that habit isn't automatically noble.

POINT 1:
Founders will fight for fair compensation for their team and routinely accept an underwhelming, unexamined number for themselves.

POINT 2:
Chronic underpayment of the founder isn't automatically virtuous - it can create real personal financial stress that quietly distorts business decisions.

POINT 3:
The founders who make clearer decisions treat their own compensation as a real number worth examining, not just whatever's left over.

CAPTION:
A founder who'll fight hard to make sure the team is paid fairly often accepts an underwhelming, barely-examined number for their own salary - whatever's left over after everything else is accounted for, treated as automatically fine simply because it's their own money.

That chronic underpayment isn't automatically virtuous. It can create real personal financial stress that quietly distorts business decisions - taking on unnecessary risk to make up the gap, or making calls colored by personal financial anxiety that has nothing to do with what the business actually needs.

The founders who make clearer decisions treat their own compensation as a real number worth examining deliberately, the same rigor they'd apply to anyone else's pay.
