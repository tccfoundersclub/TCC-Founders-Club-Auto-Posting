HOOK:
How Long Does It Actually Take a New Customer to Feel the Product Was Worth It?

INTRO:

CAPTION:
General usage metrics - logins, time in product, feature clicks - tell you someone is active. They don't tell you whether that person has actually experienced the specific moment where the product's real value became obvious to them.

The gap between signup and that first genuine "okay, this is actually worth it" moment is one of the strongest predictors of whether a customer sticks around, and it's a number most teams don't explicitly track or try to shorten.

The founders who reduce early churn treat shortening that specific gap as a real, measurable goal - not just watching general engagement and hoping value gets felt eventually.
