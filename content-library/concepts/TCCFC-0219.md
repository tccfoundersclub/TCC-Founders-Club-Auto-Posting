"Why should I join a startup instead of a stable big company" comes up in nearly every serious recruiting conversation, and a lot of founders answer it differently, and less convincingly, every single time it's asked - because they've never actually sat down and prepared a real answer.

Without that preparation, the response defaults to generic startup platitudes - "fast-paced," "real impact," "wear many hats" - that don't actually address the candidate's real, legitimate risk calculation about leaving stability behind.

The founders who recruit well have a specific, honest, well-rehearsed answer ready - tailored to what genuinely makes their company worth that particular risk, not a generic script.
