TOPIC:
Founders assume their vision is self-evident to new hires

HOOK:
The Vision That Feels Obvious to You Is Invisible to Someone Who Joined Last Month

INTRO:
Founders carry the vision constantly in their own head. Point 2 is why that closeness misleads them about new hires.

POINT 1:
A founder who's lived with the company vision for years has internalized it so deeply that explaining it can feel almost unnecessary.

POINT 2:
A new hire has none of that context, and without deliberate onboarding into the "why," they end up executing tasks without understanding the larger purpose.

POINT 3:
The founders who build genuinely aligned teams over-explain the vision to new hires, treating it as something that has to be actively taught, not absorbed automatically.

CAPTION:
A founder who's lived with the company's vision for years has internalized it so completely that explaining it out loud can start to feel almost unnecessary, even repetitive - surely everyone already gets it by now.

A new hire has none of that accumulated context. Without a deliberate effort to onboard them into the actual "why" behind the company, not just the "what" of their specific tasks, they end up executing work without understanding the larger purpose it's supposed to serve.

The founders who build genuinely aligned teams over-explain the vision to new hires, well past the point where it feels necessary to the founder personally, because it takes real repetition to become as obvious to someone new as it already is to the founder.
