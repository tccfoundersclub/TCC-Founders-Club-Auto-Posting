CAPTION:
A founder's own salary often gets set once, early on, out of necessity, and then just... stays there, unreviewed, even as the company's financial position genuinely improves and could clearly support paying the founder more fairly.

Leaving it frozen can feel like discipline and responsibility. It can also just be an unexamined habit - a number nobody's actually revisited with fresh eyes, sitting well below what the company could now reasonably support.

The founders who make clear-headed decisions about this review their own compensation periodically, with the same rigor and honesty they'd apply to approving a raise for anyone else on the team who'd earned it.
