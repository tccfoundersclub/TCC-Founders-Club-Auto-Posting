TOPIC:
Founders treat a signed contract as the end of the relationship-building work

HOOK:
Signing the Contract Is the Start of the Relationship, Not the Finish Line

INTRO:
Founders relax once a deal is signed. Point 2 is why that relaxation is premature.

POINT 1:
A signed contract feels like the win, and the actual work of proving the value promised in the sales process is just beginning.

POINT 2:
Relationship investment that drops off right after signing sends a quiet signal that the courtship is over and the real relationship was never the point.

POINT 3:
The founders who retain customers well keep investing in the relationship post-signature at least as much as they did during the sales process.

CAPTION:
A signed contract feels like the finish line - the deal is closed, the number is booked, time to move to the next thing. In reality, everything that was promised during the sales process still has to actually be delivered, starting now.

When relationship investment visibly drops off right after signing - less responsiveness, less attention, less of the energy that closed the deal in the first place - it sends a quiet, unintended signal that the courtship was the real relationship, and now that it's over, so is the attention.

The founders who retain customers well keep investing in the relationship after signature at least as much as they did during the sales process, treating the signed contract as a beginning, not a conclusion.
