Founders underestimate how much a clear "who does what" doc reduces daily friction

HOOK:
A Lot of Daily Team Friction Traces Back to a Document That Was Never Written

INTRO:

POINT 1:

POINT 2:

POINT 3:

CAPTION:
Recurring friction between two team members gets read as a personality clash more often than it should be. Frequently, it's something far more mundane: two people who are both genuinely unclear on who actually owns a specific decision or task, stepping on each other without meaning to.

Without a written answer to "who does what," that ambiguity gets resolved informally and inconsistently, case by case, which breeds a low but steady hum of tension that never quite gets traced back to its actual source.

The founders who reduce this kind of friction write down ownership clearly, even for things that feel too obvious to bother documenting - the obvious things are often exactly where the ambiguity hides.
