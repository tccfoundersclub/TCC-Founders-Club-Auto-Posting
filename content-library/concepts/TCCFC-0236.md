TOPIC:
Founders often don't know which of their habits their team has quietly adopted

HOOK:
Your Team Has Probably Copied at Least One of Your Habits, Good or Bad, Without You Noticing

INTRO:
Founders underestimate how closely they're watched. Point 2 is why that matters.

POINT 1:
A founder's habits - how they handle stress, how they communicate under pressure, how they treat mistakes - get absorbed by the team, consciously or not.

POINT 2:
That absorption happens whether the habit is a good one worth spreading or a bad one worth breaking, and founders rarely notice which is which.

POINT 3:
The founders who build the healthiest teams stay aware that their own behavior is a template being copied, and are deliberate about which parts of it they want replicated.

CAPTION:
A founder's habits - how they handle stress publicly, how they communicate when something goes wrong, how they treat their own mistakes - get absorbed by the team over time, consciously or not, simply from proximity and repeated observation.

That absorption doesn't discriminate between good habits worth spreading and bad ones worth breaking. A founder who handles pressure poorly and doesn't notice is quietly teaching the whole team to handle pressure the same way.

The founders who build the healthiest teams stay aware that their own everyday behavior is functioning as a template being copied, and are genuinely deliberate about which parts of it they'd actually want replicated.
