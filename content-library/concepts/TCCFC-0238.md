TOPIC:
Founders underinvest in making their own onboarding as a new hire's manager clear

HOOK:
You Onboard Customers Carefully. Do You Onboard as Someone's Actual Manager Just as Carefully?

INTRO:
Product onboarding gets real design attention. Point 2 is the equally important onboarding that doesn't.

POINT 1:
A lot of thought goes into how a new customer experiences their first weeks with the product. Far less thought goes into how a new hire experiences their first weeks with the founder as their manager.

POINT 2:
Without deliberate design, that management relationship forms by accident, shaped by whatever happens to come up, rather than by real intention.

POINT 3:
The founders who build strong early relationships with new hires treat the first weeks of being managed by them with the same design care as product onboarding.

CAPTION:
Real thought and design goes into how a new customer experiences their first weeks with the product - a deliberate flow, clear expectations, a plan for what "good" looks like. Far less thought typically goes into how a new hire experiences their first weeks being managed directly by the founder.

Without that same deliberate design, the actual management relationship just forms by accident - shaped by whatever happens to come up in those early weeks, rather than by real intention about what the new hire needs to feel supported and set up well.

The founders who build the strongest early relationships with new hires bring the same design thinking to their first weeks of being managed that they'd bring to a customer's first weeks with the product.
