A big, generous gesture - covering someone's costs, a surprise bonus, a grand public thank-you - is memorable, and it doesn't compensate for a steady pattern of small broken commitments: the late reply, the missed follow-up, the call that gets rescheduled one too many times.

Real trust is built cumulatively, through small, consistently kept promises, far more than through occasional grand ones. The small stuff is actually the foundation - the big gestures are just decoration on top of it.

The founders who are trusted most reliably by their team and their network keep their word on the small, unglamorous things consistently, understanding that this - not the occasional grand gesture - is where trust actually gets built.
