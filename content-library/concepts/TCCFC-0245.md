TOPIC:
Founders overestimate how much their team wants to be consulted on every decision

HOOK:
Not Every Team Member Wants to Be Consulted on Every Decision. Some Just Want Clarity.

INTRO:
Founders sometimes over-consult out of a desire to be inclusive. Point 2 is why that can backfire.

POINT 1:
Consulting the team broadly on every decision feels inclusive and can, for some people, just feel like extra unwanted responsibility without real authority attached.

POINT 2:
Some team members genuinely prefer clear direction over being asked their opinion on decisions outside their actual scope.

POINT 3:
The founders who lead well learn which decisions genuinely benefit from wide input and which ones the team would rather just be told clearly.

CAPTION:
Consulting the whole team broadly on every decision feels inclusive and respectful, and it doesn't land the same way for everyone. For some people, being asked their opinion on decisions well outside their actual scope just feels like extra unwanted responsibility, without any real authority attached to it.

Plenty of team members genuinely prefer clear, confident direction from the founder over being consulted on things they don't feel equipped or interested to weigh in on - being asked isn't automatically experienced as empowering.

The founders who lead well learn to distinguish which decisions genuinely benefit from wide input and which ones the team would honestly rather just be told clearly, without confusing the two.
