CAPTION:
A founder's default mode in almost every conversation - with the team, with investors, with customers - requires projecting competence and confidence. That's genuinely necessary for the role, and it's also a kind of constant performance that takes something out of a person over time.

Without at least a couple of relationships where that polished exterior can actually drop - where "honestly, I'm struggling with this" is welcome, not a liability - the constant performance becomes quietly isolating, even while surrounded by people every day.

The founders who stay psychologically healthy through the hard stretches protect at least one or two relationships specifically for this, and actually use them, rather than letting the performance become the only mode they know how to operate in.
