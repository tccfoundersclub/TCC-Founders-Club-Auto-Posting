TOPIC:
Founders assume a good hire will naturally figure out unwritten cultural norms

HOOK:
Your Unwritten Cultural Norms Are Invisible to Everyone Except the People Who Already Know Them

INTRO:
Founders assume culture is absorbed naturally. Point 2 is why new hires often miss it entirely.

POINT 1:
A lot of real culture lives in unwritten norms - how decisions actually get made, what's genuinely valued versus what's just stated - invisible to anyone not already inside it.

POINT 2:
A new hire without access to those unwritten rules can misread the culture entirely, or violate a norm nobody thought to explain because it felt too obvious.

POINT 3:
The founders who onboard people successfully into real culture make an effort to articulate the unwritten rules explicitly, not just the official values on a slide.

CAPTION:
A meaningful amount of real company culture lives in unwritten norms - how decisions actually get made day to day, what's genuinely valued versus what's just stated on a values slide, which rules are strict and which are more like guidelines. None of that is visible to someone who isn't already inside it.

A new hire without access to those unwritten norms can misread the culture entirely, or unintentionally violate a norm that nobody thought to explain simply because it felt too obvious to the people who already knew it.

The founders who successfully onboard people into the real culture, not just the stated one, make a genuine effort to articulate the unwritten rules explicitly, treating them as important information to share, not something people should just naturally absorb.
