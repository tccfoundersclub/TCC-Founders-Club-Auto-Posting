INTRO:

CAPTION:
Noticing a team member is struggling, a founder's instinct is often to jump straight to offering solutions - reassignment, extra time off, a pep talk - based on a reasonable guess about what would help.

That guess is frequently wrong, or at least incomplete, because what someone genuinely needs in a hard moment isn't always what looks obviously helpful from the outside looking in. Sometimes it's space. Sometimes it's more direction, not less. Sometimes it's just being asked.

The founders who support their teams well ask directly - a simple, genuine "what do you actually need right now" - instead of assuming they already know the answer and acting on a guess.
