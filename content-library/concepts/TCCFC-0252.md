Founders often don't have a clear answer for what makes their company worth betting a career on

HOOK:
Could You Explain, in One Clear Sentence, Why Someone Should Bet Their Career on You?

INTRO:
Founders assume the pitch covers this. Point 2 is why the two questions are actually different.

POINT 1:

POINT 2:

CAPTION:
A company pitch explains the market opportunity, the product, the trajectory. A strong candidate deciding whether to join is actually asking a different, more personal question underneath all of that: is betting my career on this specific team, at this specific moment, actually a smart move for me.

Without a clear, honest, specific answer to that personal version of the question, candidates are left to fill in the gap with vibes - genuinely useful signal, and not a substitute for a founder who can articulate it directly.

The founders who recruit the best people have thought through and can clearly say why this specific bet is worth someone's career, not just why the market opportunity is large.
