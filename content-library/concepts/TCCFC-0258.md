CAPTION:
Enormous energy and focus go into the push toward a big milestone - a launch date, a funding close, a revenue target - and comparatively little thought goes into what actually happens the day after it's hit, once the immediate goal is gone.

That gap can create a genuinely disorienting letdown right at the moment that's supposed to feel like the biggest win - all that focused energy suddenly has nowhere obvious to go, and the absence of the next clear target can feel strangely deflating.

The founders who sustain real momentum have some sense, even a loose one, of what comes immediately after the milestone before they actually reach it, so the win doesn't leave a vacuum right behind it.
