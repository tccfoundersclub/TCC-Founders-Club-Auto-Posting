TOPIC:
Founders sometimes conflate a customer's enthusiasm in a sales call with actual buying intent

HOOK:
An Enthusiastic Sales Call Isn't the Same Thing as a Deal That's Actually Going to Close

INTRO:
Founders read energy in a call as a strong signal. Point 2 is why that energy can mislead.

POINT 1:
A genuinely energetic, positive sales conversation can reflect real interest, and it can also just be social warmth that doesn't translate into an actual purchase decision.

POINT 2:
Founders who over-index on call energy build unreliable pipeline forecasts, mistaking politeness and enthusiasm for real buying signals.

POINT 3:
The founders who forecast accurately look for concrete next-step commitments - not just enthusiasm - as the real signal of genuine intent.

CAPTION:
A sales call that feels energetic and positive - lots of nodding, genuine-sounding excitement, warm language - can reflect real buying interest, and it can just as easily be ordinary social warmth that has nothing to do with an actual decision to purchase.

Founders who over-index on that energy when forecasting build unreliable pipelines, repeatedly mistaking politeness and conversational enthusiasm for genuine buying signals, and then get surprised when deals that felt certain quietly stall.

The founders who forecast accurately look past the energy in the room to concrete next-step commitments - a scheduled follow-up, a specific timeline, an actual internal champion doing something on their end - as the real signal of genuine intent.
