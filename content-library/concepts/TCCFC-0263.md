TOPIC:
Founders often haven't tested what their product looks like to someone using an old phone or slow connection

HOOK:
Your Product Works Great on Your Phone. Have You Tried It on a Three-Year-Old Phone With a Bad Connection?

INTRO:
Founders test on their own good devices and connections. Point 2 is the real-world gap that creates.

POINT 1:
A founder's own phone, laptop, and internet connection are usually newer and faster than a meaningful share of the actual customer base.

POINT 2:
Performance and usability issues that never show up in a founder's own testing can be a real barrier for customers on older hardware or weaker connections.

POINT 3:
The founders who build genuinely accessible products test deliberately on constrained conditions, not just their own comfortable setup.

CAPTION:
A founder's own phone, laptop, and internet connection are almost always newer, faster, and more reliable than a meaningful share of the actual customer base - which means the product genuinely never gets tested under the conditions a real chunk of users are actually experiencing it in.

Performance issues, slow load times, or clunky interactions that never once show up in a founder's own comfortable testing setup can be a real, silent barrier for customers on older hardware or a weaker connection - invisible to the person building it, obvious to the person using it.

The founders who build genuinely accessible products test deliberately under constrained conditions - an old device, a throttled connection - not just their own best-case setup.
