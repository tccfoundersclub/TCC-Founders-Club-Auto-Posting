TOPIC:

HOOK:
A Well-Attended Webinar Doesn't Automatically Convert Proportionally to How Many People Showed Up

INTRO:
Attendance numbers get used as a proxy for expected results. Point 2 is why that proxy is unreliable.

POINT 1:
A large audience shows genuine top-of-funnel interest, and conversion from attendance to real action depends on completely separate factors.

POINT 2:
Founders who plan around attendance-proportional conversion are often surprised when a big turnout produces a modest result.

POINT 3:
The founders who run effective events measure and plan around actual downstream conversion, not attendance size as a stand-in for it.

CAPTION:
A large, well-attended webinar or event genuinely shows real top-of-funnel interest - people showed up, which matters. Whether that turnout converts into anything further depends on completely separate factors: the offer, the follow-up, the specific ask made at the end, none of which scale automatically with headcount.

Founders who plan around an assumption that conversion scales proportionally with attendance are often genuinely surprised when a big turnout produces a modest, underwhelming result on the other side of it.

The founders who run genuinely effective events measure and plan around actual downstream conversion directly, treating attendance size as one input among several, not a reliable stand-in for the real outcome.
